The market and its turbulent state

How to read it: the top line is ₹1 in the Indian market; the shaded line below is the chance that the market was in its most volatile state that day. Real time uses only the days up to each date, which is all an investor could have known; with hindsight uses the whole period, including the future, so it looks sharper than anything usable in practice.

The states

How to read it: each state’s average return and volatility a year, how many days it lasts on average, and its share of days. The fitted model is from the years you picked, so these numbers describe those years, with hindsight.
